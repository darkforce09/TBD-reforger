## Approach
1. Teach `smokes.rs` PERF_PROBE to read `window.__t9382` and assert on residency allocations per pan,
   not on chunk-URL fetch counts. Prove non-vacuity by perturbation: force an allocation, watch it go
   red, restore, `touch`, green.
2. Decide and record how a slice worktree reaches the everon build input — a read-only bind/symlink
   of `staging/`, a `--staging` path override on `build-world-objects`, or an operator step per wave.
   This half may be `executor: human`; say so on the ticket rather than pretending an agent can do it.

## Risks
- A counter that reads zero because its subject disappeared is the repo's signature defect. The new
  assertion must fail loudly when there is nothing to measure, never pass quietly.
- `smokes.rs` is large and shared; keep the change to the probe.
