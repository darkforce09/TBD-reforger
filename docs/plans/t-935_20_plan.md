## Approach
1. Delete the two modules and their declarations; follow the compiler.
2. Move each of the eleven readers onto the index, or delete the gate that only existed to check a
   chunk partition. `schema_gates.rs:4044` goes **silently vacuous** under `if catalog.exists()` —
   fix that, do not let it pass quietly.
3. Strip chunk vocabulary from `chunk_math.rs`, `world/index.rs`, `manifest.rs`, `chunk.rs`,
   `trace.rs` and `density_ladder.rs`, or justify each surviving name in a comment.
4. Perturbation: restore one chunk-grid lookup on the trace path; its test must go red.
