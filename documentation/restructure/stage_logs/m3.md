## S12 playtest: what to look at per kind

M3 moves each objective kind's rules into its own behaviour class and changes no rule, so a round
should look exactly as before. Check, per kind, on a mission with one zone of each type:

- **All kinds, at load:** the server log prints one `objective` line and one `objectiveRules` line
  per objective with that kind's rules, and the `built` line with the capture, destroy and hold
  counts; an inert objective prints its reason.
- **Capture (`objective_capture`):** standing inside builds the capture bar for the players inside;
  an enemy inside pauses it and the board shows CONTESTED (`contestable`); an empty zone holds or
  decays by `onEmpty`; taking an enemy-held zone first neutralises it, then captures; a `CAPTURED`
  chat line on capture; a `startingOwner` zone starts held; `all_objectives_captured` ends the round
  when one side owns every usable capture zone. Board glyph `o`, `+` or `-` by owner.
- **Destroy (`objective_destroy`):** the targets are found on the first live tick (else the objective
  goes inert with a logged reason); the board shows `intact n/m`; a `DESTROYED` chat line when
  `targetCount` targets are destroyed; `objective_destroyed` ends the round for the zone's side.
  Board glyph `#`.
- **Hold until (`objective_hold_until`):** the board shows `hold Ns left`, PAUSED while an enemy is
  inside (`pauseOnEnemy`), restarting with `resetOnEnemy`, counting only with a holder inside when
  `requireHolderPresent`; hold-clock log rungs at 600, 300, 120, 60, 30 and 10 s; a `HELD` chat line
  and `hold_expired` ends the round for the holder; a `startingOwner` other than the holder is
  logged and ignored. Board glyph `H`.
- **Known behaviour to note, not a regression:** F-M3-02 (an `objective_complete` trigger without
  `zoneId` never fires while a capture objective exists).
