## BLOCKER-1 (T-644) — the viewshed wash renders vertically MIRRORED: the encoder is row-0-south, the textured pipeline is row-0-north, and the forest-density model it cites y-flips where the viewshed does not

**Evidence.** Three links, each verified in source:
1. **The shader puts texture row 0 at world NORTH.** `shader.wgsl` `vs_textured`: `out.uv = vec2(in.unit.x, 1.0 - in.unit.y)` with the comment "North-up: unit.y=1 (world maxY = north) → v=0 (texture top). Mirrors `lanes::corner_uv`" (shader.wgsl:52-57); `lanes::corner_uv` + its `corner_uv_is_north_up` test pin the same contract (lanes.rs:112-118, 144-149). `viewshed_upload` draws through this exact pipeline — the `BatchPayload::Textured` dispatch routes every role except `ForestFill` to `textured_pipeline` (engine.rs:974-981), and `write_texture` lays the buffer's first `bytes_per_row` at texture row 0.
2. **The encoder puts world SOUTH first.** `compute_viewshed`'s `idx_of` maps `r = round((y − min_y)/cell)` (sample.rs) — `cells[0..cols]` is the `min_y` (south) row — and `encode_viewshed_rgba` iterates `vs.cells` in that order (los_tool.rs:138-144); `pack_rgba_256` pads rows in place without reordering. Its own doc claims the opposite of the shader: "the engine's `world_rect_rel` + its `flip_y:false` upload convention put world-min at texture-row-0, matching the basemap/forest-density lanes" — **false on both counts**: there is no flip_y parameter anywhere on this path, and both cited lanes are row-0-north.
3. **The model the slice says it copied flips; the copy doesn't.** `forest_density_upload`'s feeder is `pack_island_r8_yflip` — `let tex_row = (ISLAND_CORNERS - 1) - gy` (density_island.rs:74-89) — and the basemap is row-0-north by contract ("TBDS block-row-0-north, Rust hillshade row-0-north all agree", lanes.rs:113). The slice reproduced the lane's plumbing (own texture, world-rect quad, 256-pad, Nearest sampler) and dropped the flip.

Net: the first bytes uploaded are the raster's SOUTH row; the shader renders them at the rect's NORTH edge. The wash is reflected about the rect's horizontal midline.

**Impact.** Every asymmetric verdict paints on the wrong side of the observer: dead ground computed behind a ridge NORTH of you is shaded SOUTH of you (at the mirrored ridge position), and vice versa; where the disc is coast-clamped (asymmetric rect) even the visible center displaces. On flat/open ground the wash is uniformly transparent and the bug is invisible — which is why a casual smoke pan would pass it — but on the live Everon DEM essentially every real observer near terrain renders wrong dead ground. A planner-facing overlay that misinforms is worse than one that is absent; the ticket's acceptance ("shade every cell it can and cannot see") is false as rendered. No pin catches it: the palette/encoder/pad tests are all raster-space, the engine has no orientation test, and the compute goldens never touch the GPU path.

**Disposition.** Document only (factory rule). Fix shape for the owning wave: 2 lines — iterate rows reversed in `encode_viewshed_rgba` (or flip in `pack_rgba_256`), mirroring `pack_island_r8_yflip`; then correct the false "flip_y:false / world-min at row-0" doc sentence, and add the missing bridge pin: encode a 1×2 raster (one Hidden north cell, one Visible south cell) and assert the FIRST texel row is the NORTH cell — i.e. tie the encoder to `corner_uv`'s contract the way `corner_uv_is_north_up` ties the shader.
